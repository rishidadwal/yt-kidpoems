# Video Design
- **Format:** 1280×720 @ 25 fps (main video) · 1080×1920 (Short, only if the poem runs 58 s or less) · H.264 + AAC 160k.
- **Look:** soft pastel toddler cartoon, the same main character in every verse, and no text inside the images.
- **Lyrics:** bottom-centred. The current word fills yellow (#FFD700), karaoke `\kf` style, with a thick pink (#DD2A7B) outline and a shadow. Font size is 7.5% of the height (landscape) or 4.5% (Short).
- **Timing:** a 1.2 s title card, then each line with a 0.45 s pause, then a 2.5 s "♪ ♥ ♪" ending.
- **Motion:** slow Ken Burns zoom (1.00 ↔ 1.15) per verse.
- **Audio:** voice rate −10% and pitch +15 Hz. Music at 12% volume. Target loudness −16 LUFS (to build).
- **Thumbnail:** 1280×720, the first scene plus the title in 96 px white with a 10 px pink outline, in the language's own script.
- **Accessibility:** high-contrast text. No flashing faster than 3 Hz.
